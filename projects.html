Things I've built

Three live client websites built at Artic9: what each one does, its key features, and where to see it.

Wellness equipment retailer · Nagercoil

Theosis Health & Care

A multi-page website for Theosis Health and Care, a Nagercoil-based wellness brand with experience centres across Tamil Nadu and Kerala. It presents the product range and makes it easy for visitors to book a free demo by phone or WhatsApp.

Key features

  • Product catalogue with category browsing, individual product pages and a downloadable brochure.
  • “Book a demo” and “Free demo” calls to action linked to WhatsApp and phone.
  • Experience-centre information with city pages and a full location list.
  • About page with company story, timeline, mission and vision, and FAQ.
  • Floating contact and social shortcuts for quick enquiries.
  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
Visit theosis.in ↗

Medical college & hospital · Kanyakumari

Sudha Saseendran Siddha Medical College

A multi-page institutional website for Sudha Saseendran Siddha Medical College and Hospital (Mecode Vaidhyasalai). It serves prospective students, staff and patients, with the B.S.M.S. programme, departments, facilities and hospital information organised into clear navigation.

Key features

  • Home page hero slider with an announcements ticker and news and events sections.
  • Dropdown navigation across About, College, Facilities, Hospital and Explore sections.
  • Department directory with English and Tamil subject names.
  • Course page covering duration, recognition and affiliation of the B.S.M.S. programme.
  • Facilities, hospital services, gallery and downloadable documents.
  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
Visit sudhasaseendransiddha.org.in ↗

Building materials & sanitaryware retailer · Tamil Nadu

PIPES N TILES

A multi-page website for PIPES N TILES, a building-materials and sanitaryware retailer with five showrooms across Kanyakumari and Tirunelveli districts. It showcases the product range and lets visitors explore the showroom virtually before getting in touch.

Key features

  • Product catalogue across eight categories, including sanitary ware, taps, tiles, pipes and kitchen fittings.
  • 360° virtual showroom view.
  • Showroom directory for five branches with map links and contact details.
  • Gallery, brand and channel-partner showcase, and customer testimonials.
  • Brochure download plus WhatsApp, phone and email enquiry options.
  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
Visit pipesntiles.in ↗
